# k-star privacy policy

Updated 15 October 2026.

## Privacy contact

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email the developer of k-star at kallaroo.indie@gmail.com.

## About this policy

This policy covers the k-star Android puzzle app, including development and preview builds. The developer of k-star is responsible for the information they handle through the app and support correspondence. You can play without creating an account.

Your puzzle progress and settings stay on your device. Advertising, purchases and Android diagnostics involve the providers described below. Internal builds may use Google test ads, which still contact Google’s services.

## Your puzzles and settings

Puzzle entries, cross marks, highlights, move history, elapsed play time, progress, statistics, preferences and cached Premium status are stored on your device to save your game and choices. Elapsed play time is saved even when the timer is hidden. The app also stores records of which puzzles you have started and when. Completion counters and the last interstitial time determine when an ad is eligible.

Puzzles are included with the app. There is no k-star account, cloud-save service or custom gameplay analytics service. The app does not upload your grids or puzzle history to the developer. Android backup and device-transfer services may copy app data according to your device and Google account settings.

## Advertising and your choices

When advertising is enabled, the free version uses Google AdMob for banners on puzzle-selection pages and interstitial ads after eligible puzzle completions, before the Solved summary. An interstitial is eligible after three completed puzzles or three minutes since the last interstitial. It never interrupts an unfinished puzzle.

Google and its advertising partners may process your IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, Android advertising ID, app set ID and other device or account identifiers, app launches, taps, ad views and diagnostic information. These are used to deliver and personalise ads where permitted, measure usage and ad performance, and prevent fraud. App set ID is used for non-advertising purposes such as fraud prevention. This advertising measurement is separate from your locally stored puzzle history. The information used depends on your device, location, SDK configuration and privacy choices.

In builds using the developer’s AdMob configuration, Google’s consent service manages applicable regional choices. Settings → Privacy options lets you revisit them when available. Internal builds using Google’s demo ad IDs may not show a consent form. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Non-personalised ads can still involve data processing for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Premium stops future in-app ads. It does not delete information already received by an advertising provider or disable other services described in this policy.

## Purchases

Google Play processes the one-time Premium purchase. The app receives purchase status and a purchase token to check ownership through Google Play and complete the transaction. It does not receive your full payment-card details.

Purchase tokens are not saved in the app’s database or sent to a developer-operated server. Premium status, product identifier and verification time are saved locally. Restore purchase checks purchases through the Google Play account on your device. Google keeps its own payment and purchase records.

## Crash and diagnostic information

When you allow your Android device to share usage and diagnostics information with Google, Android and Google Play may collect crash, app-not-responding (ANR) and other app-quality information. The developer uses available Android vitals reports in Google Play Console to investigate faults and improve stability. Availability depends on your device, diagnostic-sharing settings and installation through Google Play.

You control this sharing through your device’s Usage & diagnostics settings; wording and availability vary by device. Google handles this information under its own policies. These settings are separate from the advertising choices described above.

The app includes no additional crash-reporting SDK and does not send custom crash reports or puzzle history to a developer-operated service. It does not enable screen recording or gameplay analytics.

## Contact and sharing

If you email the developer, they and their email provider handle your email address, message and any attachments to respond to you. Google provides the Gmail service used for the privacy contact listed here. Please avoid including sensitive information that is unnecessary for your enquiry.

Share this app opens your device’s sharing options with the app name and store link. It does not include puzzle progress. Your chosen sharing service handles anything you send through it. Links to stores or other websites are subject to those services’ privacy policies.

## Keeping and deleting information

Local puzzle progress and statistics remain until you reset them or clear the app’s data. Resetting selected puzzles or difficulty groups deletes their progress and statistics. Settings → Reset all puzzles deletes all puzzle progress and statistics, including retained completion records, while keeping preferences, Premium status, puzzle-start records and ad-eligibility counters.

To remove all local app data, use Android Settings → Apps → k-star → Storage → Clear storage or Clear data. The wording varies by device. Backups are separate: Android may restore backed-up data later, so check your device and Google backup controls as well. The developer cannot remotely view or erase progress stored only on your device.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and related follow-up, or meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion using the privacy contact.

Resetting puzzles or clearing local data does not delete Google’s purchase records, backups or information already received by other providers. Google and other providers retain information under their own policies and settings. Use their controls for information they hold independently.

## Security and overseas processing

Local app data is protected by your device’s app storage and access controls. The app does not add a separate layer of database encryption. Services used for advertising and purchases use encrypted network connections. No device or service can guarantee absolute security.

Google and email providers may process information outside your country. Their privacy policies describe international processing and safeguards.

## Privacy requests and complaints

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your local privacy regulator. Use the privacy contact to make a request or complaint about information the developer holds. Describe your concern and the information or correspondence involved.

The developer will review your request or complaint and reply, asking only for details needed to locate the information and verify the request. If information must be retained or a request cannot be fulfilled, the response will explain why. You can also contact your local privacy regulator.

Use Google’s controls for your Google account, purchases, backups and advertising choices. A parent or guardian can contact the developer with a privacy concern about a child.

## Changes to this policy

The policy and its update date will be revised when the app’s data practices change. Material changes will be described in the app or its release information, with new consent requested where required.
